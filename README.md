# SparTahanan

**A Student Accommodation Guide to Dormitories and Boarding Houses Near BatStateU**

**Live website:** [spartahanan.vercel.app](https://spartahanan.vercel.app)

SparTahanan is a student-focused accommodation directory that helps Batangas State University students find and compare dormitories and boarding houses near their campuses. It puts the details students care about in one place: the distance to campus, the monthly rent, what the rent covers, the room setup, and how to contact the owner.

*For the Red Spartan community. Not an official BatStateU service.*

## Pages

| Page | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Introduces SparTahanan, shows featured accommodations, and lists the BatStateU campuses with places nearby. |
| **Find Accommodation** | The directory of dormitories and boarding houses. Each listing shows the rent, price basis, distance to campus, room setup, and amenities, and **View Details** opens its full information. |
| **List Your Property** | A form for dormitory and boarding house owners to describe their space, rental rates, and room details. |
| **About & Contact** | Explains what SparTahanan is and how it works for students and owners, answers common questions, and has forms for inquiries, listing reports, and feedback. |

## Campuses covered

Alangilan, Pablo Borbon, Lipa, ARASOF-Nasugbu, JPLPC-Malvar, Lima, Balayan, Lemery, Rosario, San Juan, Mabini, and Lobo.

## How it works

**For students**
1. Choose your BatStateU campus.
2. Compare listings by rent, room setup, and amenities.
3. Contact the owner to confirm the details and arrange a viewing before paying anything.

**For property owners**
1. Describe your accommodation, its address, and the campus it serves.
2. Add the rental details: rent, price basis, deposits, and utilities.
3. Review the listing so students see accurate information.

## Project status

The website is built with **HTML and CSS only**. Because of limited time, we did not add JavaScript, so the pages show the design and content, but the search filters and forms do not work yet.
