Finding a room means weighing the commute, the cost, and how well a space fits your routine. SparTahanan puts those details side by side.

How SparTahanan works

For students

  1. Choose your BatStateU campus. Look for rooms close to where you study.
  2. Filter and compare listings. Check rent, availability, and room features.
  3. Contact the owner. Confirm details and arrange a viewing.
Find Accommodation

For property owners

  1. Describe your accommodation. Include the address, campus, and room setup.
  2. Add rental details and photos. Show what tenants get and what they pay for.
  3. Review your listing. Check that costs, occupancy, and house rules are accurate.
