How SparTahanan works

For students

  1. Choose your BatStateU campus. Look for rooms close to where you study.
  2. Filter and compare listings. Check rent, availability, and room features.
  3. Contact the owner. Confirm details and arrange a viewing.
Find Accommodation

For property owners

  1. Describe your accommodation. Include the address, campus, and room setup.
  2. Add rental details and photos. Show what tenants get and what they pay for.
  3. Review your listing. Check that costs, occupancy, and house rules are accurate.

Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered.

Send an inquiry
What is SparTahanan?

It is a student accommodation directory focused on dormitories and boarding houses near BatStateU.

Who can use the directory?

Students looking for a room can browse the directory. Dormitory and boarding house owners can use the property form.

How do I find accommodations near my campus?

Choose a campus on Find Accommodation, then narrow your search by area, budget, or distance.

Are listed rental prices monthly?

Yes. Check the price basis and any separate charges for utilities, deposits, or services.

How do I contact a property owner?

Open a listing and choose Contact Owner to see its contact details. This directory currently contains fictional listings with sample contacts.

How do property owners submit an accommodation?

Use List Your Property to enter the rental details, add photos, and review the listing. Demo submissions are saved in the browser.

Are listed properties verified?

The properties shown here are fictional examples. For a real rental, confirm the terms and condition directly with the owner before paying.

Listing guidelines
